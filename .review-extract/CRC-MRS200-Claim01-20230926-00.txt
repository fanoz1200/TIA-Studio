1) Introduction
1.1.Overview
1.1.1 CRC-DORRA was awarded the Works for the construction and finishing of Marassi Siena Villas at
Alamein with total Contract amount of (534,731,239 L. E) on Lump Sum basis (except the earthwork, stone
columns, infrastructure and landscape are subject to remeasurement according to Contractual B.O.Qs.)
1.1.2 The Employer is EMAAR MISR for Developments, Contract Administrator is GLEEDS and the
Supervision Consultant is EHAF consulting
1.1.3 The Contract Works comprise the Construction of fully finished villas (28 villas) with all its relevant
parcel earth work, soil improvement, stone columns, infrastructure, roads, Fences, Guard House &
landscape.
1.1.4 The Works commenced on 1st of September 2022 and the Contractual Completion date is 2nd of July
2024, with contractual duration of 670 calendar days from the Commencement date
1.1.5 The Contractor duly commenced its contractual obligations as required under the Contract. However,
despite the Contractor’s best efforts and diligence, it has encountered a major delay event due to Site
obstacles and additional works which is out of CRC's scope of work include Probing points method and
filling cavities.
1.1.6 CRC has exerted its utmost efforts to mitigate the delay event negative impact by:



Starting boreholes works and submitting geotechnical report instantly, although the Site was not
ready for such works while the Site obstacles were still existing.
Increasing the number of equipment (regardless the planned numbers) to mitigate the delay caused
by the probing points method and filling cavities works that includes uncertain quantities, and
demanded uneven quantities of material supply on daily basis which was a challenge to avoid the
potential delay.

1.1.7 Based on Sub-Clause 9.2 (Possession of site) and Sub-Clauses 22.1 & 22.2 (Variations) &Sub-Clause
19.2 (Extensions of Time) of the Contract, as a result of the above event, it is submitted that CRC is
entitled to claim for an Extension of time and its associated costs. Accordingly, CRC hereby submits this
claim to reflect the impact of the above events on the works Completion Date. Otherwise, the Cost
impact shall be submitted in due course.

1.2.Reservation of Rights
1.2.1 This Claim is an interim claim that involves the retrospective events before the cut-off date (6th of June
2023) only and their prospective impact on the remaining works of the project.
1.2.2 This submission is not and shall not be construed to constitute a waiver of any of the Contractor’s
rights, benefits and entitlements available to it under the Contract and/or at Law. Furthermore, the
Contractor hereby reserves the right to provide at any time further and better particulars in support of this
submission and/or to amend it in any way.
1.2.3 Where there is a continuing effect of matters described within this submission and/or further events
and circumstances that may arise after the relevant cutoff date of this submission which provide the
Contractor with additional entitlement, these matters will form part of a further claim submission
