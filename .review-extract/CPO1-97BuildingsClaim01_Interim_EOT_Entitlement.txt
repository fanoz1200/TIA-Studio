Description of the Methodology (MIP 3.7) 4.1.1. The analysis follows the AACE International Recommended Practice No. 29R-03 , specifically the MIP 3.7 (Modeled / Cumulative / Past vs. Future) protocol, known as the Impacted As-Planned vs. As-Built Comparison . 4.1.1.1 Contractual Compliance with Sub-Clause 8.4 (As Amended) The delay analysis presented herein has been performed in accordance with Sub-Clause 8.4 of the Conditions of Contract, as amended in the Particular Conditions. The methodology adopted constitutes a structured time impact-based simulation carried out on the approved Baseline Programme using recognized international delay analysis practices (AACE International RP 29R-03). The analysis logically models the instructed Variations (NOV 15 and NOV 18) within the approved CPM network and evaluates their effect on the Time for Completion through longest path analysis and comparison with the actual as-built progress. Furthermore, the Contractor confirms that it has exercised its best endeavours to mitigate the delay effects through parallel work fronts, resource optimization, and resequencing across the 17 work groups, thereby reducing the theoretical modelled impact to the actual net delay demonstrated in this submissio n 4.1.2. The method utilized is the RMAS "Retrospective – Modeled – Additive – Single Base Simulation" Protocol (Longest Path Analysis), which is described as follows: Retrospective: The analysis is performed after the onset of the variation instructions (NOV 15 & NOV 18), identifying the actual and forecast impacts on the 97 buildings. Modeled: The analyst inserts " Fragnets " (sets of activities) representing the additional scope into the approved Baseline CPM network to compare the "Before" (Planned) and "After" (Impacted) states. Additive: Specific schedule elements and logic ties for the Smart Systems and Private Gardens are added to the Baseline to model the impact on the project’s critical path. Single Base Method: The additive simulation is performed on the one and only approved Baseline network analysis model, ensuring a static and transparent logic comparison. 4.1.3. This methodology is uniquely suited for this Project due to the complexity of managing 97 buildings. It allows the Contractor to demonstrate the Theoretical Impact (1,503 days) of the variations while validating the results against the Actual As-Built (319 days) , thereby quantifying the significant mitigation efforts undertaken on-site. 4.2 Validation of the Baseline 4.2.1. The purpose of baseline adjustment is to ensure the functional utility of the CPM schedule for forensic analysis, ensuring it accurately represents the original plan for the 97 buildings. 4.2.2. The validation process was accomplished as follows: Data Date: Set at the project's Notice to Proceed (NTP) with no progress data for activities prior to the delay event. Critical Path: Ensured at least one continuous critical path using the Longest Path criterion from the Start Milestone to the Final Completion of all buildings. Logic Integrity: Ensured all activities have at least one predecessor and successor (except for start/finish milestones). Scope Representation: Verified that the full original scope of 97 buildings is represented before adding the Fragnets . Calendars: Reflected actual working days as per the Contract Agreement. 4.3 Cause of Delay vs. Effect of Delay The identification of delay in this submission is driven by the nature of the event (Delay-Cause).
