Under the Contract, the timely release of IFC drawings by the Employer/Engineer is a prerequisite for the commencement of related construction activities on site, including but not limited to excavation, Soil Replacement , foundation works, and structural works for the carpark facility. The Contractor formally submitted the necessary shop drawings/comments and continuously followed up with the Engineer for design approvals within the contractual review period. Despite the Contractor’s compliance and proactive follow-ups, the final approved IFC drawings were released significantly later than the dates stipulated in the Contract and the Baseline Programme. As a result of this delay, the Contractor was unable to commence the carpark construction works as scheduled. This caused a knock-on effect on subsequent activities that are on the project’s critical path, such as structural works for superstructure elements dependent on the carpark completion. The delay was entirely outside the Contractor’s reasonable control and directly attributable to late action by the Employer/Engineer. This event has been recorded in the Contractor’s Weekly progress reports, correspondence log, and meeting minutes, clearly evidencing the sequence of requests, follow-ups, and the eventual late receipt of approved IFC drawings. Type of Delay: Excusable & Compensable (E&C). 4.2 Cause of Event The primary cause of this event was the late issuance of the approved IFC design drawings for the carpark by the Employer/Engineer. Although the Contractor complied with all submission requirements, submitted timely comments, and continuously followed up through formal letters and progress meetings, the final IFC drawings were released significantly later than the dates stipulated in the Contract and baseline programme. This delay was attributable to extended internal reviews and design coordination outside the Contractor’s control. 4.3 Effect of Event Due to the late IFC release, the Contractor was unable to commence carpark-related construction activities such as excavation, Soil Replacement, foundation works, and structural works on the scheduled dates.
