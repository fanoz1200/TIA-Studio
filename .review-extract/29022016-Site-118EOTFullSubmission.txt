Synopsis

2.1

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

2.1.1. Al Arrab Contracting (the “Contractor”) has entered into an Agreement with Ministry of
Interior Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (the “Employer”) on 23rd June 2013 (the “Contract”) for the
engineering, procurement, construction, start-up and commissioning of some elements of KAP4
project (the “Works”) the sum of Where CONTRACTOR shall achieve COMPLETION by the
SCHEDULED COMPLETION DATE, Which is 720 Calendar Days for each site starting from taking
over the site. The Engineer of the Contract means “PROGER”, appointed by the Employer to act
as Engineer for the purposes of the Contract.

2.1.2. The Contract entered into by the Contractor was a “bid and build” Contract based upon
“Concept” status design included within the Contract. The Contractor’s design obligations extend
to development the design, the production of shop drawings and co-ordination drawings, which
are to be prepared in accordance with the Contractual design intent and other requirements as
set out within the Specification and other Contract Documents. The Contractor has no right to
vary the adaption of the site, without the express instruction of the Engineer.

2.1.3. The works comprise the Engineering and Detail Design (where ever applicable),
Procurement, Construction, Installation, and Testing, Pre-Commissioning & Commissioning (the
“Works’) for the Completion of Work at the following eight (8) CONSTRUCTION SITEs, (the
“Sites”) locations identified within kingdom of Saudi Arabia; Two Dormitories, Special Security
Forces, Three Sports Training Center, Security Command Facilities and General Security at Riyadh,
Khobar, Arar,Tabuk and Jeddh in KSA.

2.1.4. The purpose of this analysis is to perform a Longest path delay analysis of the
“Dormitories Site in AL Riyadh - Site 118” in Riyadh -Saudi Arabia “the project” performed by Al
Arrab Contracting Co for the “Ministry of Interior-MOI”, the original contract required that the
project to be completed in 720 calendar days (CDs).

recommended in AACE protocol.
